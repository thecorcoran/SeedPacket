How to Fold Your Tapeless Envelope

Designed to lock together securely and protect seeds with no adhesives, tape, or glue.

1

Cut Along Solid Outline

Carefully cut around the solid outer perimeter using scissors. (No internal hole-punching or craft knives required!)

2

Fold Left and Right Flaps Inward

Crease both side flaps inward along the dashed vertical lines toward the back of the envelope.

3

Fold Bottom Flap Up & Interlock

Fold the bottom flap up along the dashed horizontal crease over the two side flaps. Slide the two locking side ears behind the folded side flaps into the side creases. This locks the back flat and creates an airtight seed pocket!

4

Insert Seeds & Tuck Top Flap

Drop your cleaned seeds into the pocket. Fold the top flap down and tuck the rounded tongue deeply into the pocket opening.
