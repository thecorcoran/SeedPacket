How to Fold Your Tapeless Envelope

Designed to fold and lock together securely with zero glue, tape, or scissors required!

1

Fold Side Flaps Inward

Lay sheet printed-side down (or face up). Fold the left and right side flaps inward along the dashed vertical lines.

2

Fold Top Corners (Tongue)

Fold the top two corners inward along the 45° diagonal dashed lines to form a tapered triangular closure tongue.

3

Fold Bottom Up to Form Pocket

Fold the entire bottom flap up along the dashed horizontal crease. This forms a deep, secure seed pocket with solid fold seams along the sides and bottom. Drop your seeds inside!

4

Fold Top Down & Tuck In

Fold the top flap down along the horizontal crease and tuck the triangular tongue firmly into the bottom pocket. Your seeds are completely sealed!
